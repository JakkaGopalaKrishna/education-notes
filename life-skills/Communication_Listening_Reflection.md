# Communication & Listening Reflection

## Question 1: What are the steps/strategies to do Active Listening?

- Focus on the speaker and topic.
- Avoid interrupting the speaker.
- Avoid distractions from my own thoughts.
- Write down important points.  

---

## Question 2: According to Fisher's model, what are the key points of Reflective Listening? (Write in your own words, use simple English)

- Listen carefully to the speaker’s words.
- Paraphrase what i understood in my own words.
- Check with the speaker if my understanding is correct.
- Remain neutral and avoid judging. 

---

## Question 3: What are the obstacles in your listening process?

- If i know the topic well, i feel overconfident and stop listening.  
- When i’m tired i can't concentrate.  
- Difficult words makes me hard to understand. 

---

## Question 4: What can you do to improve your listening?

- Avoid overconfidence and listen.  
- Stay active and fresh with proper rest.  
- Improve language understanding by asking doubts. 
- Take notes to stay attentive.

---

## Question 5: When do you switch to Passive communication style in your day to day life?

- When talking to managers.  
- When I want to avoid conflict.   
- In new environments.  
- When the issue is not important to me.  

---

## Question 6: When do you switch into Aggressive communication styles in your day to day life?

- When I am in stressed.  
- When someone repeats the same mistake.  
- During unwanted arguments.  
- When I feel disrespected.  

---

## Question 7: When do you switch into Passive Aggressive (sarcasm/gossiping/taunts/silent treatment and others) communication styles in your day to day life?

- When I’m angry but avoid direct conflict.  
- When I feel hurt.  
- When I feel ignored.   

---

## Question 8: How can you make your communication assertive? You can watch and analyse the videos, then think what would be a few steps you can apply in your own life? (Watch the videos first before answering this question.)

- Practice active listening.   
- Use respectful tone.  
- Express thoughts clearly.  
- Maintain eye contact and confident body language.  
- Control emotions before reacting.
