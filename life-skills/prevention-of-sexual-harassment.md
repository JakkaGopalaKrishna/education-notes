# Prevention of Sexual Harassment

### Question 1:
What kinds of behaviour cause sexual harassment?  
Sexual harassment includes any unwelcome behaviour of a sexual nature that makes a person feel uncomfortable, or unsafe at the workplace.
- Sexual comments or jokes.
- Unwanted physical contact.
- Unwelcome sexual advances.
- Requests for sexual favours.
- Sending inappropriate messages, or images.
- Bullying or intimidation with sexual undertones.

### Question 2:
What would you do in case you face or witness any incident or repeated incidents of such behaviour?  

- If I feel safe to do so, I would tell the harasser that their behaviour is unwelcome and must stop.
- I would keep a record of what happened, including dates, times, locations, and any witnesses.
- I would report the harassment to my supervisor, human resources department, or any designated authority in my organization.
- I would talk to trusted colleagues, friends, or family members for emotional support and advice.
- I would follow up with the authorities to ensure that appropriate action is taken and that the harassment does not continue.
