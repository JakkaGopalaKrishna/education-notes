# Focus Management

### Question 1
What is Deep Work?

- Deep Work was introduced by Cal Newport.
- Deep Work is the ability to focus on work without distraction.
- It helps to learn faster and improve skills.

---

### Question 2
According to author how to do deep work properly, in a few points?

- Focus on one task at a time.
- Schedule deep work time.
- Eliminate distractions, such as social media and email.
- Take breaks and rest to maintain focus and productivity.

---

### Question 3
How can you implement the principles in your day to day life?

- Set specific times for deep work and stick to them.
- Keep phone away or turn off notifications.
- Make it a daily habit.
- Take short breaks.

---

### Question 4
What are the dangers of social media, in brief?
- Social media can be addictive and lead to wasting time.
- It can cause stress and anxiety.
- It can negatively impact your mental health.
- It can lead to a decrease in productivity and focus.
