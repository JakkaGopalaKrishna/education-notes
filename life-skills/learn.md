## How am I Applying Each and Every Concept in My Life?

### 1️⃣ Learning Process (Feynman Technique, Active/Diffuse Thinking, 20-Hour Rule)

* I explain coding concepts (like callbacks, promises, MERN concepts) in simple words as if teaching a beginner.
* I study in focused 25-minute sessions (active mode) and take short breaks to allow diffuse thinking.
* When learning a new topic (like file system or backend), I break it into small parts and practice consistently.
* I focus on completing at least basic practice hours before judging myself.

---

### 2️⃣ Grit

* I continue solving coding problems even when they feel difficult.
* I don’t quit after errors — I debug patiently.
* I stay consistent in daily learning instead of expecting instant results.

---

### 3️⃣ Growth Mindset

* I believe my programming skills improve with practice.
* I accept feedback from seniors and code reviews positively.
* I see mistakes as learning opportunities, not failures.
* I continuously explore new tools and technologies.

---

### 4️⃣ Internal Locus of Control

* I take responsibility for my progress instead of blaming time, teachers, or environment.
* If I don’t understand something, I revise or ask doubts.
* I focus on effort, discipline, and attitude.

---

### 5️⃣ Communication & Listening

* I practice active listening in meetings and discussions.
* I avoid interrupting and try to paraphrase what I understood.
* I work on being assertive instead of passive or aggressive.
* I control my emotions before responding in stressful situations.

---

### 6️⃣ Tiny Habits & Atomic Habits Concepts

* I attached coding practice to a fixed daily time (after dinner).
* I start with just 10 minutes to reduce resistance.
* I celebrate small wins by pushing code to GitHub.
* I reduce bad habits (phone scrolling) by keeping my phone away at night.
* I focus on identity change: “I am a consistent developer,” not just “I want to code more.”
