## Question 3: What are active and diffused modes of thinking?

**Active Mode:**\
This mode is used when you concentrate on something specific that you are already somewhat familiar with, where your thoughts travel along tightly packed and well‑established neural pathways.

**Diffuse Mode:**\
This is a relaxed and resting state of the brain, then we can think about something else, and we can understand new concepts and discover new solutions to problems.
