# Grit and Growth Mindset

## Question 1: Paraphrase (Summarize the Video)
Grit means having passion to achieve long-term goals. Success comes from consistent effort and not giving up, even when progress is slow or challenges arise.

---

## Question 2: Paraphrase (Summarize the Video)
A growth mindset is the belief that abilities and intelligence can improve through learning and effort.

---

## Question 3: What is the Internal Locus of Control? What is the key point in the video?

**Internal Locus of Control:**  
It is the belief that your success or failure depends mainly on your own actions and decisions, rather than external factors.

**Key Point in the Video:**  
- To stay motivated.
- Avoid blaming external factors.
- Focus only on what you can control like your efforts and attitude.

---
